The harbour at low tide: a case study in access planning

How one small port made its harbour safe to visit at every state of the tide.

At low tide the old slipway left visitors stranded on the mud. The plan added a floating pontoon, clear signs and a tide board at the car park. Complaints fell to zero in the first season.
