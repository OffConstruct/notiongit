Keeping a commonplace book of client notes

The notebook that keeps twenty years of advice findable.

Date
3.11.2025
Reading
1 min
Tags
practice, notes

A commonplace book is a notebook of things worth keeping. Mine holds every lesson from every project.1 Here is how I keep it.

The rules

  1. Write the date before anything else.
  2. Copy the source with every note, even a page number.
  3. Never delete an entry; cross it out instead.

Project - the client and the year, at the top of the page.

Lesson - one sentence I would tell the next client.

A worked example

Project Lesson Notes
Port Ellery, 2024 signs before pontoons cheaper and faster
North Quay, 2025 talk to the fishermen first they know the tide
Salt Cove, 2023 one map, not five everyone reads it

Advice is only useful if someone can find it again.

When the notes moved to a computer I wrote a tiny script to rebuild the index:

2024  Port Ellery  signs before pontoons
2025  North Quay   talk to the fishermen first
2023  Salt Cove    one map, not five
