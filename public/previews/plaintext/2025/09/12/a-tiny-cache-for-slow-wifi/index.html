A tiny cache for slow harbour Wi-Fi

The ferry terminal's Wi-Fi drops every few minutes, so the timetable page now works offline.

September 12, 2025 #web #notes

The ferry terminal has one Wi-Fi access point and four hundred passengers. I added a small offline cache to the timetable page so it keeps working when the connection drops. It took forty lines and saved a lot of shouting at the ticket desk.
