Keeping a commonplace book of customer calls

2025-11-03 · 1 min read notes customers

A commonplace book is a notebook of things worth keeping. Mine is every customer call since day one.1 Here is how I keep it useful.

The rules

  1. Write the date before anything else.
  2. Copy the source with every note, even a page number.
  3. Never delete an entry; cross it out instead.

Date - first, before the harbour’s name.

Ask - what they asked for, in their words.

A worked example

Harbour Asked for Notes
North Quay refunds built in March
Port Ellery a printed list built in May
Salt Cove dark mode not yet

Your customers will tell you what to build if you write it down.

When the notes moved to a computer I wrote a tiny script to rebuild the index:

2025-03-02  North Quay   refunds        built
2025-05-11  Port Ellery  printed list   built
2025-06-19  Salt Cove    dark mode      open

  1. It is now a spreadsheet, but I still call it the book. ↩
