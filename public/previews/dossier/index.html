Profile

I write about tides, tools and the slow work of keeping things running. Most of what I publish starts as a note in a pocket book and ends up here, a little longer and a little more careful. Read the archive or start with what I am doing now.

About

I grew up between a boatyard and a public library, and I still split my week the same way: mornings at the desk, afternoons fixing whatever is broken. My essays have appeared in The Salt Review and in the anthology Low Water.

Details

  • Based in: a harbour town on the north coast
  • Writing: essays, field notes, the occasional review
  • Currently: a book about repair, due next spring
  • Contact: rowan@example.com
