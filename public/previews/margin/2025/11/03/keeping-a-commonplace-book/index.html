Keeping a commonplace book of type

November 3, 2025 Filed under notes and type

My notebook of letters seen around town, with the index that keeps it useful.

A commonplace book is a notebook of things worth keeping. Mine is full of letters: painted signs, cast-iron numbers, tickets.1 Here is how I keep it.

The rules

  1. Write the date before anything else.
  2. Copy the source with every note, even a page number.
  3. Never delete an entry; cross it out instead.

Sketch - draw the letter, then write where you saw it.

Index - the last four pages, by street.

A worked example

Letter Where Notes
R the ferry office painted, 1950s
7 mooring post 7 cast iron
& the bakery gold leaf

Good lettering is everywhere once you start looking.

When the notes moved to a computer I wrote a tiny script to rebuild the index:

2025-11-03  R  ferry office   painted
2025-11-04  7  mooring post   cast iron
2025-11-06  &  bakery window  gold leaf

  1. I started it on a rainy holiday and never stopped. ↩
