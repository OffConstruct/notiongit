About

I grew up between a boatyard and a public library, and I still split my week the same way: mornings at the desk, afternoons fixing whatever is broken. My essays have appeared in The Salt Review and in the anthology Low Water.

Details

  • Based in: a harbour town on the north coast
  • Writing: essays, field notes, the occasional review
