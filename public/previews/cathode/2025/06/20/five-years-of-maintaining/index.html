Five years of maintaining an open-source library

> What I would tell myself on day one: say no early, write the docs first, and take holidays.

Tidewatch turned five this spring. Here is what I learned, in the order I learned it.

  1. Say no to features early, kindly and in writing.
  2. Write the guide before the code.
  3. Take holidays. The library will survive.
