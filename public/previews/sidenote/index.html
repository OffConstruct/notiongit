Rowan Vale

Postdoctoral researcher in coastal hydrology. I study how harbours breathe.

Abstract

I study how coastal aquifers respond to tides and storms, pairing a century of hand-copied harbour records with a small network of new sensors. I am a postdoctoral fellow at the North Coast Institute for Marine Science, working with the Estuaries Group. Read the notes or the publications.

Research

My thesis reconstructed groundwater levels under a tidal town from 1890 to 1910 using the harbour office’s own tide tables. Current work asks how those levels change when the sea wall is repaired, and who notices first.
