A ledger of small repairs

One year of fixing things, written down: what broke, what it cost, what it taught.

20 September 2025

1 min read

essays, repair

For one year I wrote down every repair I made. There were one hundred and four. Most took less than ten minutes. Three took most of a weekend.

The ledger is not interesting because of the repairs. It is interesting because of the pattern: things break where people touch them.
